## Appeal: Guideline 4.1(c)

We are appealing this rejection. "OllamaChat" uses "Ollama" descriptively to indicate compatibility with the Ollama platform — not to impersonate the brand. Our app clearly identifies as an independent client, and our privacy policy states: "OllamaChat is an independent client application. It is not affiliated with Ollama, Inc."

**Precedent:** "Reins: Chat for Ollama" (id6739738501) is currently approved and available on the App Store, also using "Ollama" descriptively. We ask that the same standard be applied consistently.

**Ollama is open-source** (MIT license). Using an open-source project's name to indicate compatibility is standard practice.
