# Privacy Policy for OllamaChat

**Last updated: May 19, 2026**

## Data Collection

OllamaChat does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal data to us. All your data stays on your device and your own servers.

## How the App Works

- OllamaChat connects to **your own Ollama server** that you configure. We do not operate or have access to your server.
- Chat messages are sent directly from your device to your Ollama server. We never see, store, or process your messages.
- Web search (via Tavily) sends your search queries directly to the Tavily API. We do not intercept or store these queries.

## Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored **locally on your device only** and is never sent to us:
- Your Ollama server URL and API key
- Your Tavily API key
- Chat history and conversation settings

## Third-Party Services

- **Ollama API** — Your self-hosted AI server. See [ollama.com](https://ollama.com) for their privacy policy.
- **Tavily API** — Optional web search. See [tavily.com](https://tavily.com) for their privacy policy.

## Children's Privacy

OllamaChat is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted here with an updated date.
